Antioch

Also known as Antiochia ad Orontem, Antioch on the Orontes, Theopolis, Antakya

Capital city Turkey Antakya, Hatay Province, Turkey

Capital of the Principality of Antioch and one of the great cities of the medieval Mediterranean. The First Crusade took the city on 3 June 1098 after an eight-month siege, and Bohemond of Taranto — who had arranged the entry of his men through a tower held by the Armenian guard Firouz — kept it for himself, founding the principality that ruled northern Syria for 170 years. Antioch fell to the Mamluk sultan Baibars on 18 May 1268 in one of the most destructive sacks of the era, and the city never recovered: by the fifteenth century only a few hundred inhabited houses stood within the walls. Stretches of the fortification walls climbing the mountains east of the city, several aqueducts, and the Cave Church of St Peter survive; most of the ancient city lies buried beneath Orontes river sediment.
